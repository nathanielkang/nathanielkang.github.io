SOCAL Lab · Kyungpook National University

Research

The lab develops statistical methods for tabular data across three lines of work: federated learning when tables cannot be pooled, imbalanced regression when the rare target matters, and AI alignment for deployed language models.

Theme 01

Federated learning & privacy

When tables cannot leave a site, we develop statistical methods that share compressed, privacy-preserving summaries and synthesize usable tables without pooling the rows.

Orchestrating Federated Tabular Synthesis at Scale with Fault-Tolerant Pipeline Execution

Neurocomputing

Each client keeps its table and sends only compressed, noise-perturbed column summaries across an institutional boundary. A coordinator holds the party list, checkpoints, and analysis workload, then feeds the aggregated summaries to a synthesis engine built from a diffusion model, a workload-guided loss, and a cached set of those summaries. A quality monitor scores how close the synthetic table is to the real one, how well it supports downstream tasks, and how well it matches the workload; if a stage fails, checkpoints let the run resume instead of starting over.

Theme 02

Imbalanced regression

A loss averaged over the whole sample is dominated by the dense part of the target. We develop statistical methods that reweight rare regions and generate additional tail rows so imbalanced tabular regression does not ignore the cases that matter.

Taming the Tails: Why Distributionally Robust Optimization Needs New Theory for Imbalanced Regression

NeurIPS 2026 · Poster

A single robustness radius is usually set by the dense region, so ordinary DRO still underfits the tail. TC-DRO partitions the target into bins and lets that radius grow as local density falls. Inside each bin it solves a small dual problem that assigns a training weight to every sample. Rare bins receive weights larger than the usual uniform weight, and the model is trained by weighted stochastic gradient descent.

Conditional Diffusion Models for Imbalanced Tabular Regression

UAI 2026 · Poster

A relevance score marks target values that the training density underrepresents. The denoiser is conditioned on the target (scale-and-shift of its features), and that score multiplies the noise-prediction loss. To generate data, the method draws a rare target, runs the reverse diffusion process, mixes the new rows with copies of real rare rows, and keeps the highest-scoring candidates.

Theme 03

AI alignment

A language model inside an institution is not one threat model. Accountability and the timing of data exposure should change the controls. We organize attacks and defenses by those settings instead of treating every deployment as a generic chatbot.

Not All Large Language Model Deployments Are Created Equal: A Taxonomy-Driven Survey of Security, Defense, and Governance

Under review · Journal of Information Security and Applications

The taxonomy crosses two axes: who the user is accountable to, and whether sensitive data are exposed only at training time or continuously at inference. The four cells are a secured repository, a managed pipeline, a fortified service, and a contested interface. Attacks and controls are evaluated in that cell.
