Theme 01

Federated learning & privacy

Parties keep their tables. What moves is a compressed summary, not the rows.

Orchestrating Federated Tabular Synthesis at Scale with Fault-Tolerant Pipeline Execution

Neurocomputing

Each party agent sends compressed, noise-perturbed marginals across a trust boundary. A coordinator holds the party registry, checkpoints, and workload, and passes the aggregated marginals to a synthesis engine: a diffusion model, a workload-guided loss, and a marginal cache. A quality monitor scores total variation, downstream utility, and workload error, and can request a retry. Checkpoints let a failed stage resume without restarting the run.

Theme 02

Imbalanced data

A single loss, averaged over the whole sample, is dominated by the dense part of the target.

Taming the Tails: Why Distributionally Robust Optimization Needs New Theory for Imbalanced Regression

NeurIPS 2026 · Poster

A single robustness radius is set by the dense region. TC-DRO bins the target and lets the radius grow as local density falls. The dual is solved inside each bin and returns a weight on every sample. Rare bins receive weights above the empirical-risk weight of 1, and training is weighted stochastic gradient descent with those weights.

Conditional Diffusion Models for Imbalanced Tabular Regression

UAI 2026 · Poster

A relevance function marks target regions the empirical density under-represents. The denoiser is conditioned on the target by a scale-and-shift layer, and the noise-prediction loss is multiplied by that relevance weight. Generation draws a rare target, runs the reverse process, mixes those rows with copies of real rare rows, and keeps the highest-scoring candidates.

Theme 03

AI alignment (LLM)

A language model in an institution is not one threat model. Who is accountable, and when data are exposed, changes the controls.

Not All Large Language Model Deployments Are Created Equal: A Taxonomy-Driven Survey of Security, Defense, and Governance

Under review · Journal of Information Security and Applications

The taxonomy crosses who the user is accountable to with whether sensitive data are exposed at training time or continuously at inference. The four cells are a secured repository, a managed pipeline, a fortified service, and a contested interface. Attacks and controls are read against that cell, rather than against a generic chatbot.
