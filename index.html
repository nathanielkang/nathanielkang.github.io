About the lab

The SOCAL Lab, led by Nathaniel Kang in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Kyungpook National University, develops statistical tools and methods for tabular data. While vision and language receive substantial attention, tabular domains remain underexplored despite presenting steep practical challenges: sample sizes are often small, columns combine categorical and numerical features, and values are frequently missing. To address these difficulties, we design statistical methods—including synthetic data generation and federated learning—tailored to settings where sensitive tables cannot be pooled across sites, regression targets concentrate in rare tail distributions, and deployed models require dependable AI alignment.

We are actively recruiting motivated Master's and Ph.D. students. If you are interested, please review our Prospective Students page and contact natekang@knu.ac.kr.
