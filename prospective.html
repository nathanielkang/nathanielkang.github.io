Overview

Mission and fit

SOCAL Lab develops statistically principled methods for tabular data where standard ML pipelines fail: decentralized and privacy-sensitive records, imbalanced and long-tailed targets, and LLM accountability. We consider motivated Ph.D., M.S., and undergraduate researchers who combine careful theory with clear scientific communication and are prepared for a selective inquiry process.

SOCAL Lab은 표준 기계학습으로 다루기 어려운 정형 데이터 — 분산·프라이버시, 불균형·꼬리 구간, LLM 책임성 — 에 대한 통계적 방법을 연구합니다. 이론과 과학적 글쓰기를 함께 갖추고, 선별적 문의 절차에 응할 석·박사 과정 및 학부 연구생을 검토합니다.

Research areas

Representative directions

01

Federated learning (data privacy)

Collaborative learning when raw records cannot be centralized, with emphasis on statistical behavior under client heterogeneity and privacy constraints.

  • Federated optimization and aggregation under non-IID partitions and distribution shift.
  • Privacy-preserving federated protocols (e.g., differential privacy, secure aggregation, and risk-aware design) and their interaction with model utility.
  • Communication- and computation-aware federated training under realistic deployment constraints (dropouts, stragglers).
02

Synthetic data

Generation and augmentation of tabular data when sensitive tables cannot be released, including federated and differentially private synthesis. This line is also how the lab attacks imbalanced regression: extra rows in the tail, evaluated beyond headline averages.

  • High-fidelity tabular synthesis with semantic or structural constraints (including LLM-assisted constraint discovery where appropriate).
  • Federated or cross-party synthesis preserving correlations while limiting disclosure of raw private tables.
  • Augmentation and resampling to improve coverage in low-density / tail regions, with evaluation beyond headline averages.
03

AI alignment (LLM)

Evaluation- and governance-oriented work on large language models in institutional settings, emphasizing measurable risk and accountability.

  • Privacy-aware evaluation and auditing workflows for deployed or procured LLM systems.
  • Security, defense, and governance perspectives on heterogeneous LLM deployments (structured surveys and taxonomies).
  • Policy–implementation gaps in public-sector technology procurement and post-deployment accountability.

Expectations

Mindset and preparation

Mindset and conduct. Members should be self-motivated and determined: research progress depends on sustained initiative through ambiguous phases. Communication skills matter—clear writing and spoken explanation of assumptions, limitations, and findings are part of the research product.

연구 자세. 불확실한 구간에서도 스스로 밀어붙이는 태도가 필요합니다. 가정의 한계와 결과를 글과 말로 분명히 설명하는 것도 연구의 일부입니다.

Technical preparation. Strong probability and statistics, and scientific programming (for example Python), are required for most projects. Experience in machine learning, numerical linear algebra or optimization, and deep learning helps, depending on the research area. You should be comfortable reading primary papers and implementing ideas on your own.

기술 준비. 확률·통계와 Python 등 프로그래밍은 대부분의 과제에서 필수입니다. 기계학습, 수치 선형대수·최적화, 딥러닝 경험은 주제에 따라 도움이 됩니다. 원문 논문을 읽고 아이디어를 스스로 구현할 수 있어야 합니다.

Collaboration. Members are expected to work collegially and to respect privacy and data-use agreements in applied work.

협업. 동료와 맞춰 일하고, 응용 과제에서는 프라이버시와 데이터 사용 약속을 지킵니다.

Funding

Financial support

Support depends on fit and degree level. Eligible members may receive Brain Korea (BK) scholarship support and PI personal research funding (tuition and living on funded lines, per university and grant rules). PI personal research funding backs research assistantships on active projects—not a guarantee for all applicants.

지원은 적합성과 학위 과정에 따라 달라집니다. 선발된 구성원은 규정 안에서 BK 장학금과 교수 개인 연구비(등록금 및 생활비)를 받을 수 있습니다. 교수 개인 연구비는 진행 중인 과제의 RA에 쓰이며, 지원자 전원에게 보장되지 않습니다.
